# Sony BRAVIA, Android TV 8 to 10 and Google TV 11 to 14.
#
# Apply:   scripts/debloat.sh debloat/sony-bravia.txt
# Preview: scripts/debloat.sh --dry-run debloat/sony-bravia.txt
# Undo:    scripts/restore.sh
#
# Nothing is uninstalled, only disabled. Packages your model doesn't have are skipped, so this one
# list covers every BRAVIA generation. Put a # in front of any line you want to keep.
#
# Based on the Sony lists tested on real TVs in CleanYourTV (MIT, github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv/
# tree/main/examples), keeping only the entries that showed up on many models.
#
# Never in this list, and refused by debloat.sh if you add them: com.sony.dtv.tvx (Live TV and every
# HDMI input, which Shelf's Inputs button opens), the com.sony.dtv.tvinput.* services, Bravia Sync,
# the Sony settings apps and com.android.providers.tv (the content in Shelf's top shelf).
# Also keep com.vewd.core.service: on some models com.sony.dtv.airplayapp crash-loops without it.

# Viewing tracker (ACR: fingerprints what's on screen, HDMI included)
tv.samba.ssm
tv.samba.ssm.ui

android.autoinstalls.config.sony.bravia        # installs partner apps through Play
